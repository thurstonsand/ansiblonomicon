# Air, water, and comfort

- bathroom exhaust fans: the first attempted replacement exposed that the existing fan has no duct behind it. A professional advised replacing the fan in place rather than opening the finished roof; the roofer separately advised against adding a roof penetration before reroofing. Accept the existing condition, install the replacement fan, and revisit proper exterior ducting when the roof is replaced in roughly 5–10 years. The toilet-room pick remains the Panasonic WhisperFit DC in the [lighting buy list](../research/lighting-trial-buy-list.md)
- basement dehumidifier: current one is small; it has a hose drain (nice). Get a bigger one and understand what ongoing maintenance it still needs
- under-sink cold-water filter installed
- the replacement refrigerator filter was misplaced; buy another rather than allowing that to stall the change
- powder-room toilet intermittently leaks or runs continuously, draining water until it is adjusted. First determine whether water is escaping internally into the bowl or externally, then repair the responsible flapper or flush-valve seal, chain, fill valve, supply connection, or tank seal rather than accepting repeated adjustment as the operating procedure
