# Lighting primer: fundamentals, indirect fixtures, Yuji vs Hue

## Question

Research input for [Lighting design](04-lighting-design.md). Three questions against primary sources (lighting design references, manufacturer specs, not blogspam): (1) the fundamentals of residential lighting that make a house feel good — layering, color temperature, CRI, glare, indirect vs recessed, and why all-cans reads badly; (2) the Hue indirect/wall-wash product family (Signe, Play, Gradient) — real specs, brightness, and whether the promotional look holds up per reviews with measurements; (3) **Yuji** high-CRI bulbs — what they actually deliver vs standard LEDs and vs Hue, and whether "quality of light" and "smart color" can coexist in one fixture strategy. Include outdoor lighting basics (dark-sky-friendly, motion vs dusk-to-dawn).

## Resolution

[Lighting primer](../research/lighting-primer.md) recommends separately controlled ambient, task, and accent layers instead of another ceiling grid. Use high-fidelity fixed-white light such as Yuji for visual work, Hue wall wash and gradient fixtures for mock-up-verified accents, and shielded warm exterior lighting controlled by need rather than left bright all night.
