# Electrician scope of work

## Question

One consolidated conversation with an electrician. Known items so far; more will accrue from other tickets (cooktop, lighting, bidets, cameras):

- ethernet in just about every room of the house; there's effectively none now (design in [Ethernet, rack, and cellular](02-ethernet-rack-cellular.md))
- ethernet drops for ubiquiti cameras if installed internally, and for a PoE doorbell ([Cameras and doorbell](09-cameras-and-doorbell.md))
- outlets at all toilets for bidets ([Bidets](17-bidets.md))
- outlet on the RHS of the master bath sinks so there's one on either side
- outlet in the foyer where the catch-all table goes ([Coffee station and foyer storage](13-coffee-station-and-foyer.md))
- outlet inside the second-floor closet for the Litter-Robot
- two outlets in the garage that support car charging, one on either side; outlets, not hardwired EVSE
- a light in the pantry closet; battery is the fallback, but if the electrician is already in for everything else, wire it
- **pending the lighting wiring audit** ([Lighting design](04-lighting-design.md)): any new boxes or drops the lighting plan needs, starting with a junction box or outlet in the master closet for hardwired shelf/rod task lighting
- 50A circuit to the cooktop location ([Cooktop and hood](03-cooktop-and-hood.md))
- lighting: get help ([Lighting design](04-lighting-design.md))
- bathroom exhaust fan upgrades / quieter fans ([Air, water, and comfort](14-air-water-comfort.md))

Decide: one electrician for everything vs specialists; sequencing against other trades (hood ductwork, drywall for ethernet).
