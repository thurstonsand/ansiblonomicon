# Loch Highland Atlas

## Question

Interactive annotated plans of the house: every outlet, ethernet and coax drop, light, switch, fan, and hardwired appliance, existing or planned, with the breaker that feeds it; plus the property survey for outdoor lighting and sprinklers. Read-only on a phone, edited on a desktop. Feeds the [electrician scope](01-electrician-scope.md), the [lighting wiring audit](04-lighting-design.md), and the [sprinklers](10-sprinklers.md) ticket.

## Resolution

Lives in its own repository and wayfinder: [thurstonsand/loch-highland-atlas](https://github.com/thurstonsand/loch-highland-atlas) (private), deployed at `loch-highland-atlas.lakebed.app`. The domain vocabulary, design doc, and execution map are all there; this ticket is the pointer.
