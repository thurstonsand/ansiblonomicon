# Sit-stand desk

## Question

Looking for a new sit-stand desk. **Deskhaus** keeps coming up as a great source; open to options. Bound the field with primary sources (manufacturer specs, not affiliate roundups): Deskhaus (Apex Pro / their frames), Uplift V2, Ergonofis, Branch, Secretlab Magnus Pro, Herman Miller/Fully successors, and any frame-only + custom-top route. Compare on: frame stability at standing height (wobble is the usual failure), lift capacity for a dual-monitor + eGPU workstation, height range, motor noise/speed, memory presets, warranty, top options (solid wood vs laminate) and sizes, cable management, price. Note lead times and return policies. Output: `research/sit-stand-desk.md`, verdict-first comparison; decision stays with the user.

## Resolution

[Research completed](../research/sit-stand-desk.md). The stability-first choice is a Deskhaus Apex Pro frame with a 60 x 30 hardwood top; UPLIFT V2 Commercial is the lower-risk complete-desk alternative where its crossbar, configurator, and free 30-day returns matter more. Secretlab wins cable management, while Ergonofis, Branch, and the Herman Miller-sold Jarvis do not justify their stability, price, or warranty tradeoffs for this workstation.
