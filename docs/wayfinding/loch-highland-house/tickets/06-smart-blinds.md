# Smart blackout blinds

## Question

Living room blackout blinds, to the extent possible: skylights plus a weirdly shaped window. Had Lutron before — worked, but mega expensive. Want alternatives with rock-solid connectivity (Thread/Matter? Zigbee? proprietary hub?), skylight-capable, blackout-capable. Bound the field with primary sources; measure the windows before pricing.

## Resolution

[Smart blackout blinds research](../research/smart-blinds.md) recommends trialing one SmartWings Matter-over-Thread blackout shade for ordinary rectangular windows, using model-matched VELUX or tracked SmartWings/Somfy systems for skylights, and quoting Hunter Douglas or a full-line Lutron/Somfy dealer only after identifying the odd window's geometry. Lutron remains the reliability baseline; IKEA, Eve, SwitchBot, and Serena cannot solve skylights or non-rectangular openings.
