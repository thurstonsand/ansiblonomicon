# Basement dehumidifier sizing and maintenance

## Question

Research input for [Air, water, and comfort](14-air-water-comfort.md). Current unit is small and has a hose drain. Primary sources (manufacturer manuals, DOE/Energy Star guidance): how to size a basement dehumidifier (pints/day vs square footage and dampness), whether a whole-basement or ducted unit beats a portable, and the real ongoing maintenance for a continuously-draining unit (filter cleaning, coil, drain line, biofilm) so I know what I'm signing up for.

## Resolution

[Basement dehumidifier sizing and maintenance](../research/dehumidifier-sizing.md) recommends a current DOE-rated 50-pint ENERGY STAR portable with gravity drainage as the default for an open basement under 2,000 square feet, then escalating to a professional or ducted unit for distributed rooms, sustained severe humidity, or noise isolation. The report supplies an RH-based sizing worksheet, explains old versus post-2019 pint ratings and the roughly $1,200 to $1,800 equipment premium, and makes clear that continuous drainage removes bucket carrying, not filter, coil, drain, biofilm, leak, or humidity checks.
