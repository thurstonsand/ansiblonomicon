# Dining room table

## Question

Choose a dining room table that fits the room, household, and shared living-room/AV layout. Measure the usable footprint and circulation paths, decide the normal and extended seating capacity, then narrow the material, shape, and construction requirements before shopping.

Room & Board's Grove and Chilton are the current finalists. Chilton is the front-runner because its size fits the room better. Before selecting it, mock up chairs around the actual footprint and verify that its leg placement does not make the seats nearest the legs awkward to enter or use.
