# Smart switch and sensor landscape

_Research checked 2026-09-01 against first-party product pages, manuals, firmware notes, and specifications. Prices are current list prices before tax and shipping, not bids for a whole-house order._

## Verdict

The shipping path that demonstrably keeps smart bulbs controllable while Home Assistant is down is Inovelli Blue over Zigbee binding. The standard Blue dimmer and the Blue mmWave presence dimmer both expose Zigbee binding and smart-bulb mode; Inovelli explicitly says bound Blue devices continue working with the hub offline. The presence dimmer is shipping, but its product page still labels the product beta and its production firmware remains 1.00, with newer 1.03 firmware beta-only.

Inovelli White switches themselves are shipping Matter-over-Thread products with smart-bulb mode and firmware support for Matter's Binding cluster. Matter defines persistent unicast and group bindings, so the protocol can support direct switch-to-bulb control. Home Assistant, however, does not document or expose a way to create Matter bindings today, and Inovelli publishes White binding setup only for SmartThings and Hubitat. A White-to-bulb installation commissioned only through HA therefore does **not** have a shipping, supported "works with HA down" path. Treat that claim as aspirational until HA gains a binding director/configuration interface or Inovelli documents another tested route.

For sensing, the choices measure different things. Hue's battery sensor is conventional motion sensing and its Bridge keeps Hue lighting automation local; Hue MotionAware infers motion from Zigbee signal changes among three or four powered lights and requires the $139.99 Bridge Pro. Everything Presence Pro combines PIR, long-range static mmWave, and tracking mmWave, feeds HA locally through ESPHome over Wi-Fi or Ethernet, and needs HA running for HA automations. Inovelli's Blue presence switch has mmWave plus lux at the wall switch, but no PIR.

### White Series notes

Smart-bulb mode and binding solve separate problems. Smart-bulb mode prevents the wall switch from cutting mains power to a bulb. Binding supplies the direct digital command path. Enabling smart-bulb mode alone does not make a White switch control a Matter bulb while HA is unavailable.

The VTM31-SN is a physical dimmer for ordinary loads and can also act as a smart-bulb controller. It supports no-neutral wiring, though Inovelli requires a bypass in non-neutral installations and warns that some low-wattage LED loads may need one even with neutral. Inovelli lists Home Assistant as compatible when an appropriate Thread border router is present. Its multi-way FAQ qualifies smart-switch-to-smart-switch dimming with "your hub needs to support Matter Bindings." [Product details and FAQ](https://inovelli.com/products/thread-matter-white-series-smart-2-1-on-off-dimmer-switch).

The VTM30-SN is a neutral-required on/off switch with humidity sensing. Its manual says the firmware supports Matter binding, gives switch-to-switch and switch-to-bulb examples, and says bound devices no longer need the hub to communicate. The same section provides setup destinations only for SmartThings and Hubitat, despite listing HA as a compatible Matter controller elsewhere. That omission agrees with HA's present integration documentation rather than establishing HA binding support. [VTM30-SN manual](https://help.inovelli.com/en/articles/12566697-white-series-on-off-switch-manual).

### Blue Series notes

The VZM31-SN is the mature switch in this comparison. Inovelli advertises Zigbee binding, HA support through both ZHA and Zigbee2MQTT, smart-bulb mode, physical load dimming, and direct binding to another smart switch or compatible Zigbee bulbs. Inovelli's Blue Series overview explicitly states that bindings keep devices working when the hub is offline. The coordinator is needed to join and configure devices, but it is not in the bound command path afterward. [VZM31-SN product page](https://inovelli.com/products/zigbee-matter-blue-series-smart-2-1-on-off-dimmer-switch) and [Blue Series overview](https://inovelli.com/collections/inovelli-blue-series).

The only shipping Blue motion/presence switch found is VZM32-SN, the result of Project Linus. The project considered PIR, but the shipping specification settled on a 2T2R mmWave sensor, approximately 6 m maximum range, plus lux. It does **not** combine PIR and mmWave. Inovelli's store permits purchase, while the page still warns that the product is beta. The firmware record calls 1.00 production and 1.03 beta, with 1.03 adding mmWave stability work. That is shipping hardware with actively maturing sensing firmware, not an unreleased PIR variant. [Project Linus](https://community.inovelli.com/t/zigbee-motion-switch-project-linus-blue-series/10904), [product page](https://inovelli.com/products/zigbee-blue-series-mmwave-presence-sensor-smart-dimmer-switch), and [firmware changelog](https://help.inovelli.com/en/articles/13744028-blue-series-mmwave-presence-dimmer-switch-firmware-changelog).

### Hue boundary

Hue does not sell a special bulb with a dedicated motion detector in it. MotionAware is a Bridge Pro feature that turns a geometry of existing compatible mains-powered Hue lights into a radio-sensing area. Philips says it needs three or four lights on the same Bridge Pro, allows four areas per bridge, measures Zigbee signal fluctuations, and keeps participating lights powered. That makes it a Hue-local room-level motion system, not an occupancy/presence sensor equivalent to stationary-person mmWave. [Hue MotionAware support](https://www.philips-hue.com/en-us/support/article/motionawaretm--transform-your-hue-lights-into-motion-sensors/000011).

The physical Hue Motion Sensor requires a Hue Bridge-controlled setup. HA officially supports Hue motion sensors, including their temperature and light-level sensors, and a v2 Bridge pushes changes to HA over its local event stream. Hue lighting rules can continue on the Bridge if HA is down; HA-authored automations cannot. [HA Philips Hue integration](https://www.home-assistant.io/integrations/hue/) and [Hue Motion Sensor specs](https://www.philips-hue.com/en-us/p/hue-motion-sensor/046677570972).

### Everything Presence Pro boundary

Presence Pro is the only compared sensor that combines a fast PIR signal with both stationary-person and positional mmWave. Its documented combined Occupancy entity fuses PIR, static, and tracking detections; separate entities remain available for tuning and specialized automations. Ethernet/PoE is not the factory setup path: the device ships with Wi-Fi firmware, first provisions over BLE to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi, then can switch to Ethernet. Its official HA setup ends at the ESPHome integration, with no broker or MQTT step. [Presence Pro documentation](https://docs.everythingsmart.io/s/products/doc/everything-presence-pro-nh3fIXmow9) and [HA setup](https://docs.everythingsmart.io/s/products/doc/set-up-your-pro-device-in-home-assistant-5scEfp6EX8).

## State of Matter-over-Thread binding in HA today

Matter binding exists in the standard, but that fact does not make every controller a binding director. The CSA Matter 1.4 Core Specification defines a binding as a persistent relationship from a local endpoint to a remote endpoint or group. A privileged remote "director" writes entries into the endpoint's Binding cluster; after that, the client endpoint may initiate interactions directly with the unicast or group target. The Binding cluster replaces the analogous Zigbee binding table concept. [CSA Matter 1.4 Core Specification, sections 9.4 and 9.6](https://csa-iot.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/24-27349-006_Matter-1.4-Core-Specification.pdf).

As of 2026-09-01, Home Assistant's Matter integration documents commissioning, control, multi-admin sharing, diagnostics, and OTA updates, but no binding configuration UI, action, or supported workflow. Inovelli's own White manual likewise omits HA from its binding setup links while including SmartThings and Hubitat. The defensible operational conclusion is narrow: HA can commission and control White switches, but it cannot currently establish the direct switch-to-bulb relationship on which HA-down operation depends. The White firmware and Matter standard make that future path credible; they do not make it shipping in HA today. [HA Matter integration](https://www.home-assistant.io/integrations/matter/) and [Inovelli White On/Off manual](https://help.inovelli.com/en/articles/12566697-white-series-on-off-switch-manual).

### Existing Thread border routers

The existing Apple hardware suffices for ordinary Matter-over-Thread commissioning and operation if it is a Thread-capable model: HomePod mini, HomePod second generation, Apple TV 4K second generation, or Apple TV 4K third-generation Wi-Fi + Ethernet. HA explicitly says it can use third-party border routers such as HomePod/Apple TV merely to reach the Thread radio network; the Apple router does not need HA added to Apple Home and cannot read encrypted Matter traffic. An iPhone transfers the Apple Thread credentials into HA. [HA Thread integration](https://www.home-assistant.io/integrations/thread/) and [HA Matter integration](https://www.home-assistant.io/integrations/matter/).

SkyConnect, now called Connect ZBT-1, can also become an OpenThread Border Router, but it runs Zigbee out of the box. HAOS must install/configure the OpenThread Border Router app and switch the radio to Thread. HA documents importing an existing Apple network's credentials and adding its own border router to that network, so Apple and HA border routers can cooperate on one Thread mesh. This remains less polished than the product names imply: HA calls its own Thread/Matter border-router integration work in progress and says enabling Thread requires manual steps.

There is one concrete mixed-router limitation. HA says Matter OTA updates do not work over Apple border routers because they do not forward the needed mDNS packets. Adding HA's OpenThread border router can provide the update path, though HA's recovery procedure may require temporarily powering down Apple routers while arranging the update. That affects firmware delivery, not normal encrypted control traffic. None of these border-router choices supplies Matter binding configuration; routing and application-level binding are separate jobs. [HA Matter OTA guidance](https://www.home-assistant.io/integrations/matter/#matter-ota-device-updates) and [HA Thread integration](https://www.home-assistant.io/integrations/thread/).
