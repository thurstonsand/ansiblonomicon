# Sit-stand desk research

## Verdict

**Buy a Deskhaus Apex Pro frame and a 60 x 30 hardwood top if standing-height stability is the priority.** Its four-leg, four-motor frame directly addresses front-to-back motion, carries 600 lb, reaches 48.5 inches with a one-inch top, and has a 20-year frame warranty. The frame is $925; pairing it with a current $560 Progressive Desk 60 x 30 solid-walnut top puts the useful baseline near **$1,485 before cable accessories**. It is the strongest fit for a dual-monitor plus eGPU workstation and leaves enough capacity to stop treating every monitor-arm adjustment as a structural experiment.

**Choose UPLIFT V2 Commercial if returnability and a complete configurator matter more than maximum rigidity.** Its crossbar measurably improves side-to-side stability over the ordinary V2, while UPLIFT supplies cable management, many top materials, same-day shipping on stock configurations, a 15-year warranty, and free 30-day returns in the contiguous US. Its 48.7-inch finished maximum is slightly lower than the ordinary V2's 50.9 inches, so verify the user's standing elbow height first. UPLIFT now labels the current product V3; confirm whether a quoted order uses the legacy V2/V2 Commercial frame discussed here.

**Secretlab MAGNUS Pro wins cable management, not the desk comparison.** The concealed power leg and hinged full-width tray are excellent, but its fixed 59.1 x 27.6 metal-clad top, two-leg construction, 265 lb rating, and five-year warranty trade away flexibility and assurance. Ergonofis Sway is the polished solid-wood turnkey choice, but at $2,295 its premium buys finish and controls rather than published stability evidence. Branch and Jarvis are credible conventional two-leg desks; neither has enough first-party or methodical stability evidence to displace the two recommendations above.

Prices below are US prices observed September 2, 2026, before tax. Promotions and configurable products move often; re-price the exact cart before ordering.

## Option notes and sources

### Deskhaus Apex Pro and Apex Pro Max

The [Apex Pro product page](https://desk.haus/products/apex-pro-2) supplies the $925 price, four motors, 600 lb rating, finished height range, speed/noise, compatible top widths, and lead times. Its four-leg layout is the soundest response to front/back rocking among the candidates without adding the Max's knee-height crossbar. The [Apex Pro Max page](https://desk.haus/products/apex-pro-max) adds fixed crossbars, costs $1,500, and ships by freight. Deskhaus does not publish a repeatable directional wobble test for either, so “best” rests on frame geometry and capacity, not a directly comparable deflection number.

Deskhaus's [warranty](https://desk.haus/pages/warranty) covers Apex frames for 20 years but its HPL and custom solid-wood surfaces for one year. Its [return policy](https://desk.haus/policies/refund-policy) permits eligible like-new products in original packaging for 30 days. Solid wood and custom colors are final, while HPL and freight returns leave shipping with the buyer. That makes a bare frame safer to audition than a finished Deskhaus hardwood desk, provided every carton survives assembly.

### UPLIFT V2 and V2 Commercial

UPLIFT's legacy [specification guide](https://www.content.upliftdesk.com/content/pdfs/lookbooks/pricing-and-specification-guide.pdf) records V2 at 25.3–50.9 inches finished and 355 lb, and V2 Commercial at 22.6–48.7 inches finished and 355 lb. The Commercial adds a crossbar and larger columns. The current [standing-desk product URL](https://www.upliftdesk.com/uplift-v2-standing-desk-v2-or-v2-commercial/) now presents the V3 and starts at $599, so old V2 recommendations should not silently become a V3 purchase.

The permitted secondary evidence is BTOD's measurement work. Its [V2 test](https://www.btod.com/blog/stability-test-upliftdesk-v2/) explains the controlled top and pull forces and publishes deflection at 30, 42, and 46 inches. Its [V2 Commercial test/review](https://www.btod.com/blog/stability-test-uplift-v2-commerical/) applies the same WobbleMeter approach; the crossbar controls side-to-side wobble well, though both directions deteriorate near full extension. The apparatus is proprietary and the scores are not inches of displacement, but the fixed conditions make the within-site comparisons useful.

UPLIFT's [solid-wood desktop page](https://www.upliftdesk.com/solid-wood-desktops) shows 60 x 30 rubberwood, acacia, Saman, and reclaimed-birch choices; its configurator also includes laminate and bamboo. The [15-year warranty](https://www.upliftdesk.com/warranty) is long, although customers pay warranty shipping after year one. The [return policy](https://www.upliftdesk.com/returns/) is the best here: 30 days, original packaging, and UPLIFT-paid return shipping for eligible contiguous-US orders.

### Ergonofis Sway

The [Sway product page](https://ergonofis.com/products/sway-standing-desk) is unusually complete: $2,295 at 60 x 30, four solid-wood species, 22–48.3-inch travel with its 1¼-inch top, 360 lb advertised lift, under 40 dB, cable net, two presets per user, 5–10-business-day shipping, and a 30-day trial. Its separate [warranty](https://ergonofis.com/pages/warranty) narrows the story to 10 years on steel, five on motors/electronics, and three on wood, and excludes loads over 300 lb. Use 300 lb as the operational ceiling. No repeatable stability measurement was located.

### Branch Standing Desk

Branch's [product page](https://www.branchfurniture.com/products/standing-desk) gives the 60 x 30 laminated-MDF configuration, $899 list price, 275 lb limit, very broad 26–52-inch range, four presets, under-50 dB claim, and slow 1.3 cm/s movement. It includes a brushed grommet but not a full cable tray. Branch's [warranty](https://www.branchfurniture.com/pages/warranty) covers Standing Desks bought after January 1, 2023 for 10 years. Its [returns page](https://www.branchfurniture.com/pages/returns) allows 30 days for like-new products in original packaging, but desks pay a location-dependent label fee. The checked 60 x 30 configuration was out of stock, despite the generic page also advertising 3–5-business-day dispatch for available goods.

### Secretlab MAGNUS Pro

The [MAGNUS Pro specification and feature page](https://secretlab.co/pages/magnus-pro) provides the 59.1 x 27.6-inch dimensions, 25.6–49.2-inch range, 265 lb distributed load, three presets, five-year warranty, metal/MDF/HPL construction, powered leg, and hinged cable tray. The fixed top is shallower than the requested 30 inches and its rear tray consumes some wall clearance, so measure usable depth rather than rounding 27.6 to 30. Secretlab's [warranty portal](https://secretlab.co/pages/warranty-terms) says desks receive up to five years and generates the legal return document by purchase date. Read that generated document at checkout; these terms are less legible and less generous than UPLIFT's.

### Herman Miller / Fully Jarvis

Herman Miller is the current first-party seller of the former Fully desk. Its [Jarvis guide](https://store.hermanmiller.com/features-buying-guides-jarvis-standing-desk.html?lang=en_US) says the available ranges reach 23–51 inches and explains the current sizes: rectangular 60-inch tops are 27 inches deep, while contour bamboo supplies 60 x 30. The [bamboo product page](https://store.hermanmiller.com/standing-desks/jarvis-bamboo-standing-desk/2542428.html?lang=en_US) publishes 350 lb, 1.5 in/s, under 50 dB, and the optional four-preset OLED handset. Current frame coverage is seven years on that page, not the older Fully 15-year warranty still repeated in reviews.

BTOD's [Jarvis versus V2 measurement report](https://www.btod.com/blog/jarvis-desk-vs-uplift-desk-v2/) uses its fixed-height WobbleMeter protocol and finds both conventional two-leg desks develop wobble above roughly 43.5 inches. Herman Miller's [return terms](https://store.hermanmiller.com/customerservice-returns-exchanges.html?lang=en_US) impose a 10% restocking charge on ordinary items and exclude custom/special orders.

### Frame-only hardwood route

Two straightforward combinations avoid proprietary tops:

1. **Apex Pro + hardwood**, the recommended route. Pair the $925 frame with a local 60 x 30 x 1–1½-inch hardwood top or Progressive Desk's [60 x 30 solid walnut top](https://www.progressivedesk.com/products/walnut-wood-tabletop), listed at $559.99 with threaded inserts and dual grommets. A local top can be better, but ask the maker to flatten both faces, finish both faces and edges, and state the finished weight.
2. **Progressive Solo Ryzer + hardwood**, the value route. Its [frame page](https://www.progressivedesk.com/products/solo-ryzer-frame) lists $382.99, 270 lb, 24–49 inches, and 1.57 in/s; Progressive's [complete 60 x 30 bamboo desk](https://www.progressivedesk.com/products/solo-6030-bamboo) adds the useful specifications of under 45 dB and four memories. The frame and walnut top total about $943 on observed sale pricing. It does not solve wobble as directly as four legs do.

For either route, subtract the top, monitors, arms, eGPU, dock, mounted power strip, and cable tray from the frame rating. Even a 270 lb frame should have ample static capacity for this workstation; stability and monitor-arm leverage, not lift capacity, are the deciding loads.

## What to measure or decide before ordering

- Measure standing elbow height in work shoes and seated elbow height in the actual chair. Compare those finished desktop heights, not bare-frame travel. If standing height exceeds about 46 inches, favor four legs and treat every two-leg option as audition-required.
- Tape out **60 x 30** on the floor and desk plane. Confirm door/stair path, baseboard clearance, monitor viewing distance, and whether a rear cable tray needs extra wall clearance.
- Weigh or conservatively total both monitors, arms, eGPU/enclosure, computer, audio gear, UPS or power bricks, desktop, and every item mounted below it. Record monitor-arm clamp depth and required edge thickness.
- Decide whether true solid wood is worth seasonal movement, weight, and return restrictions. For the recommended build, choose species, 1 versus 1½-inch thickness, edge profile, finish, and grommet positions before drilling.
- Decide whether side-to-side rigidity justifies a crossbar near the shins. The Apex Pro avoids it; Apex Pro Max and V2 Commercial use one.
- Draw the moving cable system: one flexible wall lead, desk-mounted surge protection, eGPU/host interconnect, monitor leads, and enough service loop for full travel. Choose tray dimensions only after placing frame rails and monitor clamps.
- Verify the exact cart's handset, feet depth, top dimensions, stock status, freight method, return exclusions, and warranty shipping charges. Keep every carton until the desk has moved through its full range under load.
