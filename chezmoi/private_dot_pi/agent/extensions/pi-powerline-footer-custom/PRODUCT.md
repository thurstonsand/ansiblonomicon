# Product

## Register

product

## Users

A personal operations environment for one expert user managing macOS, TrueNAS, OpenClaw, Cloudflare, dotfiles, and AI-agent tooling. The user is usually working inside terminals and agent harnesses, where fast recognition matters more than broad onboarding.

## Product Purpose

This repository keeps system configuration, agent tooling, and infrastructure reproducible. Interface work should reduce operational friction: expose the few signals worth acting on, stay readable under terminal constraints, and avoid ornamental status noise.

## Brand Personality

NieR: Automata influenced, sharp, minimal, stylistic. Stoic and technical rather than friendly or playful. The mood is controlled machinery, quiet precision, and purposeful contrast.

## Anti-references

Avoid noisy status bars, dense glyph soup, decorative footer clutter, dashboard-like over-reporting, and color that competes with the work. Do not make the interface look like generic SaaS chrome, gamer RGB, or terminal maximalism for its own sake.

## Design Principles

- Signal survives first contact. Keep only information that changes decisions or orientation.
- Minimal does not mean plain. Use restraint, alignment, and typography-like rhythm to carry style.
- The terminal is the scene. Respect narrow widths, low contrast themes, and long sessions.
- Stylistic details must earn their slot. If a segment does not help, remove it.
- Prefer legible structure over clever symbolism.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal accessibility target. Favor readable contrast, avoid relying on color alone for important state, and keep motion absent unless it has a clear operational purpose.
